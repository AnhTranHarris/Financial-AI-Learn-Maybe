# M46–M55 — Event Intelligence and Macro Scenario Research

This phase teaches Dusty to use free, symbol-relevant event/news information as **conditional world-state evidence**, never as a direct trading signal.

## Permanent laws added

1. **Research universe may be broader than execution universe.** Metals, energy, crypto, futures, indices and equities may provide context without becoming tradable instruments.
2. **Economic underlier is not trading instrument.** XAUUSD, COMEX gold futures and a gold ETF may share an underlier while retaining separate market identities and histories.
3. **Automatic news acquisition is free-only and symbol-conditioned.** Paid, license-restricted, unknown, and user-provided sources never enter automatic acquisition.
4. **News updates scenarios; markets validate scenarios.** No headline maps directly to LONG/SHORT/BUY/SELL.
5. **Source repetition is not independent evidence.** Story hashes remove exact headline duplicates and publisher groups prevent syndication from masquerading as corroboration.
6. **Every scenario must be falsifiable.** A scenario needs explicit transmission channels, confirmations and invalidations.
7. **Low-liquidity accumulation is a research hypothesis, not a pre-positioning rule.** Dusty measures whether movement persists or reverses when higher liquidity returns.
8. **Free does not mean useful.** M54 measures incremental research utility and can pause a free source that fails to add value.
9. **Point-in-time truth remains mandatory.** Event capsules, news items and scenarios cannot contain information that was not knowable at the reconstruction time.
10. **Event intelligence never grants broker-write authority.** M55 can only certify readiness to continue demo-execution engineering.

## Milestones

| Milestone | Capability | Gate |
|---|---|---|
| M46 | Market/underlier identity + symbol research profile + free source registry | futures need explicit contract identity; automatic sources must have free access state and symbol relevance |
| M47 | Compact NewsItem and EventCapsule schema | timezone-aware published/known times; no full-page archive required |
| M48 | Scheduled-event reconstruction | scheduled event must be currency-relevant and point-in-time knowable |
| M49 | Unscheduled event clustering | explicit adapter-classified event keys, bounded clusters, exact headline deduplication |
| M50 | Source-independence accounting | publisher groups determine corroboration rather than raw article count |
| M51 | Event reaction/session research | bounded reaction windows and low→high liquidity continuation/reversal measurement |
| M52 | Strategy × event interaction research | bounded cells by event class, scenario state and session; one strategy per summary |
| M53 | Scenario reasoning bridge | bounded scenario EvidenceSnapshot with premise, transmission, confirmations, invalidations and corroboration |
| M54 | News value-of-information gate | source must remain free, relevant, backlog-earned and incrementally useful |
| M55 | Event-intelligence certification | all gates must pass; broker_write_authorized remains False |

Network acquisition remains outside the deterministic core. Source adapters are responsible for lawful access, normalization, event/topic classification and provenance. The core is responsible for what information is admitted, how it is bounded, how independence is counted, how scenarios remain falsifiable, and how evidence reaches reasoning.
