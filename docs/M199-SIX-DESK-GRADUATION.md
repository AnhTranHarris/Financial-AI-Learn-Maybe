# M199 — Six-Desk Graduation Protocol

M199 applies the constitutional six-desk graduation rule on top of M198 multi-desk certification.

Requirements:

- six independent certified evidence units;
- no reused certification evidence;
- one frozen Champion identity across the graduation set;
- any rejected evidence rejects graduation;
- pending evidence cannot manufacture a pass;
- sequential and concurrent execution are equivalent if identities/evidence remain independent;
- no broker-write, live-write, promotion, risk-override, or Guardian-override authority.

M199 does not redefine single-desk certification, portfolio risk, or strategy qualification. Those remain owned by earlier milestones.
