# Dusty Dragon M56-M65 — Research Reality, Capital Governance & Pre-Demo Certification

M56-M65 closes the gap between Dusty's research intelligence and any future Demo Desk engineering. This phase does **not** add MT5 order placement and does **not** authorize trading. It hardens point-in-time correctness, instrument economics, strategy representation, realistic simulation, selection-bias defenses, capital sizing, portfolio governance and evidence-backed certification.

The governing principle is:

> Research may propose opportunity. Capital management may size it. Portfolio management may allocate it. Growth management may reduce or withhold deployment. Risk and Guardian may veto it. No lower layer may enlarge the constitutional risk envelope.

## M56 — Semantic Integrity Hardening

Implemented in `core.py`, `experience.py` and `event_research.py`.

- `EvidenceItem` timestamps are timezone-aware and validated.
- Core coherence rejects evidence whose `observed_at` is later than the reasoning instant. Future information is therefore rejected at the deterministic core, not merely trusted to adapters.
- Human/public trade episodes use quantity-aware cash-flow accounting for entry, scale-in, scale-out and final exit.
- Episodes cannot over-exit, reverse quantity accidentally, or finish with unexplained open quantity.
- Event reactions can carry explicit event/observation timestamps and interval boundaries.
- Session-repricing research rejects overlapping explicit intervals rather than summing potentially cumulative returns twice.

Hard boundary: a point-in-time reasoning call cannot accept future evidence as coherent.

## M57 — Instrument Economics v2

Implemented in `markets.py` and the read-only `mt5worker.py` boundary.

`InstrumentEconomics` models broker-specific units needed for later safe sizing and realistic research:

- contract size;
- tick size and tick value;
- minimum, step and maximum volume;
- margin rate;
- commission per lot;
- long/short swap values;
- stop and freeze distances.

`BrokerSymbolSnapshot` binds those economics to a market identity, broker/account currency, capture time and leverage. The MT5 worker remains read-only and can capture symbol/account specification evidence without exposing `order_send`.

All economic values and requested volumes must be finite. Volume normalization is downward-only and asserts that rounding never increases requested risk.

Hard boundary: economic underlier, broker instrument and contract economics remain separate concepts.

## M58 — Strategy IR v2 & Eligibility Constitution

Implemented in `strategy_ir.py`.

`StrategySpecV2` is a versioned declarative strategy representation supporting:

- ALL/ANY entry rule groups;
- mandatory initial stop policy;
- target, trailing and breakeven rules;
- maximum holding steps;
- decision timeframe and intended horizon;
- session filters;
- event exclusion windows;
- cooldown;
- bounded scale-in and scale-out semantics;
- explicit execution sensitivity.

Canonical hashing normalizes clause and logically equivalent group ordering. V1 entry/cost semantics can be migrated into V2 without allowing downloaded arbitrary executable code.

The eligibility constitution prohibits deployment promotion for:

- scalping;
- HFT;
- latency-critical behavior;
- martingale;
- loss-recovery sizing;
- unbounded averaging;
- decision timeframes below M5;
- intended horizons below 15 minutes.

High execution sensitivity is research-only by default. Observed entry frequency can also disqualify a candidate whose realized behavior resembles prohibited machine-scalping even if metadata claims otherwise.

Hard boundary: backtest quality cannot override prohibited execution behavior.

## M59 — Leakage-Safe Realistic Backtest Engine v2

Implemented in `backtest.py` while preserving the older cheap screen in `research.py`.

The realistic simulator owns account economics rather than signal generation. It provides:

- chronological trade processing;
- overlapping positions;
- realized and unrealized PnL;
- explicit entry/exit/swap costs;
- mark-to-market equity;
- margin used;
- high-water-mark drawdown;
- deterministic account ledger output.

Warm-up rows can prime indicators without entering evaluation metrics.

`purged_walk_forward_ranges()` constructs bounded chronological folds with explicit purge and embargo. Embargoed observations are excluded from subsequent training, so test-boundary neighborhoods are not silently recycled as immediate training data.

Hard boundary: the fast research screen remains a screening approximation; realistic ledger results are a different evidence class and may disagree.

## M60 — Statistical Reality & Overfit Gate

Implemented in `statistical.py`.

The statistical layer includes:

- append-only SQLite trial registry containing both successful and rejected trials;
- deterministic seeded bootstrap mean intervals;
- Bonferroni family-wise p-value adjustment;
- profit-concentration measurement;
- parameter-neighborhood stability checks;
- a bounded PBO-style selection-overfit proxy using train winners versus out-of-sample candidate distributions;
- a conservative search-adjusted standardized signal score whose penalty increases with the number of tried variants.

The search-adjusted score is deliberately **not** represented as an exact Deflated Sharpe Ratio implementation. Zero-variance positive return streams are treated as suspicious rather than assigned infinite confidence.

Hard boundary: Dusty must remember how many alternatives it searched, including failures. A visually attractive backtest can fail because the evidence burden is too weak relative to search breadth.

## M61 — Money Manager

Implemented in `capital.py`.

Two sizing modes are explicitly separated:

1. `MINIMUM_LOT_STRATEGY_TEST` — research-only sizing used to test broker-feasible behavior at minimum volume. It carries no growth-capital authority.
2. `GROWTH_RISK` — stop-first percentage-risk sizing for a frozen eligible strategy.

Growth sizing computes:

`allowed loss = equity × approved risk fraction`

and estimates loss per lot from stop distance plus explicit spread, expected slippage and commission. Broker volume is rounded downward only. If minimum broker volume cannot fit the risk budget, the correct result is zero approved volume and `broker_minimum_volume_exceeds_risk_budget`.

`minimum_viable_capital()` computes the equity required for a broker minimum lot to fit a chosen risk fraction.

Hard boundary: position size is a consequence of stop distance and approved loss budget. Losses never trigger larger recovery size.

## M62 — Risk Constitution

Implemented in `risk.py`.

Default constitutional limits encode a conservative hierarchy including:

- 0.25% normal trade risk;
- 0.50% Champion soft maximum;
- 1.00% hard trade ceiling;
- symbol and portfolio heat ceilings;
- daily and weekly warning/hard-loss thresholds;
- drawdown CAUTION, DEFENSIVE, RESEARCH_ONLY and FAILED states;
- margin soft/hard ceilings;
- mandatory initial stops;
- no stop widening;
- no martingale;
- no loss-recovery sizing;
- no unbounded averaging;
- incomplete risk data fails qualification.

Outcome classification separates process quality from financial sign:

- GOOD_WIN;
- BAD_WIN;
- GOOD_LOSS;
- BAD_LOSS;
- valid/invalid flat.

A profitable rule violation remains a governance failure. A rule-following loss remains a valid observation to investigate.

Zero equity is a modeled FAILED state rather than an exception. Non-finite risk values are rejected.

Hard boundary: Risk can reduce or veto deployment; it never increases the supplied risk envelope.

## M63 — Quant Portfolio Manager

Implemented in `portfolio.py`.

The Quant PM allocates a supplied risk budget using bounded methods:

- equal risk;
- inverse volatility;
- quality/volatility/correlation score.

It applies:

- per-strategy maximum risk;
- per-symbol heat limits;
- signed factor-exposure heat limits;
- correlation pressure in the quality method;
- explicit unallocated risk when constraints prevent responsible deployment.

Post-allocation assertions verify that portfolio, symbol and factor heat were not exceeded. Numerical inputs must be finite.

Hard boundary: the Quant PM may leave capital unused. It cannot manufacture additional risk merely to reach 100% utilization.

## M64 — Portfolio Growth Manager

Implemented in `growth.py`.

Capital health states are:

- THRIVING;
- HEALTHY;
- CAUTION;
- DEFENSIVE;
- CRITICAL;
- CAPITAL_INSUFFICIENT.

Growth policy is intentionally asymmetric. Healthy growth can broaden the opportunity set, but the maximum deployment multiplier is `1.0`. CAUTION/DEFENSIVE states reduce deployment; CRITICAL or insufficient capital become research-only.

External deposits/withdrawals are separated from trading PnL.

Capital feedback follows the principle:

> A loss increases investigation priority, not position size. Profit never excuses governance failure.

Capital-compression research is earned cycle by cycle. A failed cycle repeats the same starting capital; only a passing cycle may move to a lower proposed research-capital level. Compression ladders are monotone and bounded by an explicit floor. At a given capital level, having zero eligible strategies is a valid safe outcome.

Zero equity and total-loss research cycles are recordable, deterministic failed states.

Hard boundary: growth optimization operates inside the Risk Constitution; it cannot raise constitutional percentage-risk ceilings.

## M65 — Evidence-Backed Pre-Demo Certification

Implemented in `pre_demo_certification.py`.

M65 does not certify trading profitability and does not authorize orders. It verifies that M56-M64 are represented by evidence bound to one code commit.

Each `MilestoneEvidence` requires:

- milestone identity;
- pass/fail status;
- artifact hash;
- data fingerprint;
- configuration fingerprint;
- test fingerprint;
- commit SHA.

The certification rejects missing milestones, failed milestones, duplicate evidence and commit mismatches. The resulting certification bundle has a deterministic SHA-256 identity and stable checkpoint payload.

Even a completely passing bundle returns:

`broker_write_authorized = False`

A pass means only:

`ready_for_demo_execution_engineering = True`

Hard boundary: M65 is permission to engineer the next Demo Desk phase, not permission to place an order.

## Adversarial closure tests

`tests/test_governance_invariants.py` adds deterministic boundary/grid coverage beyond example tests:

- zero-equity fail-closed behavior;
- total-loss research-cycle handling;
- rejection of `NaN`/infinite capital, economics and risk values;
- growth position sizing across a grid of equities, risk fractions and stop distances;
- proof that approved volume never rounds upward and expected loss never exceeds allowed loss;
- all portfolio allocation methods across several budgets;
- proof that portfolio, symbol and factor heat remain bounded;
- monotone capital compression across starting-capital and compression-ratio grids.

## End-of-phase authority state

M56-M65 contains no MT5 order-send adapter, no live credentials, no demo-to-live promotion, and no direct LLM/model/news broker-write authority.

The intended research-to-capital chain at closure is:

```text
hypothesis
  -> point-in-time evidence
  -> cheap screening
  -> leakage/overfit attack
  -> realistic ledger / MT5 research validation
  -> frozen eligible strategy
  -> Money Manager
  -> Quant Portfolio Manager
  -> Growth Manager
  -> Risk Constitution / Guardian veto
  -> evidence-backed pre-demo certification
```

The next phase may begin controlled Demo Desk **engineering** only after this branch is treated as a certified rollback anchor. Demo execution itself must introduce its own environment identity, session fault, order-intent, reconciliation and execution certification boundaries before any broker write can be authorized.
