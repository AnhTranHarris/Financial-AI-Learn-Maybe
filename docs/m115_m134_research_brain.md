# M115-M134 — Research Brain Tranche

Status: research-only architecture. No broker-write, entry-veto, risk-override, or
Champion-promotion authority is granted by this tranche.

## Objective

Move Dusty Dragon from a collection of strong research organs toward a compact,
restartable research organism that can learn quickly on limited local hardware.

The governing loop is:

`acquire -> forecast -> score -> intake -> screen -> experiment -> attribute -> remember -> checkpoint`

Expensive computation is earned by surviving cheaper falsification. Third-party
performance claims are never treated as alpha.

## Source firewall

Initial policies intentionally err toward manual review.

- Forex Factory Calendar structured exports may be automated after point-in-time
  archival and schema validation.
- Forex Factory Trades is crowd/context research, not a signal provider.
- Myfxbook performance is a claim/case study and cannot become a research score.
- TradingView is concept/licensing-review material; no unsupported scraping.
- QuantConnect/Quantpedia/Stonehill/education/news material are research priors.
- Trader.dev is usable only through an explicitly authorized authenticated tool.
- Vibe-Trading is already certified locally as an allowlisted research contractor.

A website can create a **proposal**, never a Champion.

## Human priors

M124 contains exactly twenty falsifiable starting priors. They cover capital
preservation, fixed risk, invalidation, no stop widening, patience, FOMO and
overtrading control, frozen evaluation definitions, regime/session dependence,
volatility-aware risk, macro-event risk, execution costs, sample size, walk
forward/OOS validation, parameter stability, forward-decay haircuts, expectancy
over win rate, tail-risk asymmetry, and true diversification.

These are hypotheses to test, not trading commandments or guaranteed edges.

## Research schools

### A1 — Edge Discovery

Cheaply falsify the existence of positive OOS expectancy, require adequate
samples, stable parameter neighborhoods, and constitutional compliance.

### A2 — Quant Profitability

Requires A1 plus positive cost-stressed expectancy, acceptable drawdown, and
walk-forward transfer.

### A3 — Profit Velocity

Requires A1 and A2. It optimizes robust expectancy per unit of research
resource/time while preserving the entry-frequency Constitution. It may never
turn speed-to-profit into overtrading or HFT.

## Resource policy

The existing Dusty resource state (green/yellow/orange/red) remains authoritative.
This tranche adds:

- RAM reserve before admitting research jobs;
- sequential model inference by default;
- fewer resident models under pressure;
- cheap screens before expensive/native tests;
- serialization of native work under pressure;
- background research yields before the deterministic core/journal.

## Qwen boundary

The initial Qwen interface is a strict parser/contract only.

Allowed:
- compare supplied evidence;
- identify contradictions;
- return RESOLVED, WAIT, NO_TRADE, or RESEARCH_REQUIRED;
- cite only supplied fingerprints;
- propose up to five bounded research actions.

Forbidden:
- broker/order tools;
- risk override;
- entry veto authority;
- Champion promotion;
- live-write authority.

The direct Ollama runtime will be integrated later only through this contract.
