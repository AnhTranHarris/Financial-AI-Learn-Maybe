# M86–M95 — Quant Forecasting and Bounded Autonomous Improvement

## Phase objective

M86–M95 closes the gap between validating a historical strategy and making a falsifiable,
point-in-time forecast. Dusty may research continuously, create bounded challengers, score forecast
distributions, run purged walk-forward tournaments, and forward-test a frozen champion. It may not
rewrite a champion in place, invent a setup, raise percentage risk to chase a goal, treat a scheduled
market closure as a fault, or grant itself live-money authority.

This phase makes the improvement loop autonomous in research and Demo evaluation, not sovereign.
Promotion remains evidence-gated and content-addressed.

## Forecast reasoning contract

Dusty forecasts a distribution, not a single magic price. For one exact forecast key it records
ordered quantiles, a median, probability of an upward move, training cutoff and validity interval.
Evaluation never pools different horizons or regimes into a flattering aggregate.

The entry hierarchy is:

1. The frozen strategy must already have a valid long or short setup.
2. The forecast and scorecard must match model, symbol, timeframe, horizon and regime.
3. The forecast must be available and unexpired at the reasoning timestamp.
4. Out-of-sample sample count and calibration must pass their predeclared thresholds.
5. Directional probability and expected edge must clear estimated round-trip costs.
6. The broker-native market clock must authorize that direction.
7. Risk must approve. No forecast can override Guardian.

Failure at any layer produces WAIT with inspectable reasons. Forecast models never call the broker.

## Market-time behavior

The schedule is captured from the connected broker through
[`SymbolInfoSessionTrade`](https://www.mql5.com/en/docs/marketinformation/symbolinfosessiontrade)
and [`SymbolInfoSessionQuote`](https://www.mql5.com/en/docs/marketinformation/symbolinfosessionquote).
Dusty also binds the terminal build, server, broker, exact symbol, MT5 trade mode and latest native
tick. Schedule snapshots are point-in-time evidence and expire; a future or stale snapshot cannot
authorize an entry.

`TRADE_RETCODE_MARKET_CLOSED` (`10018`) is expected outside a scheduled trade session. The same code
during a scheduled open session is an anomaly and fails closed. An open session also needs a fresh
native tick. Position supervision and offline research remain active while new entries wait.

Broker UTC offsets are snapshots, not permanent timezone rules. The native schedule campaign must
refresh them after broker daylight-saving or session changes.

## Capital growth without risk inflation

Dusty treats growth as breadth, not leverage pressure:

- realized settled gains can unlock more independently certified opportunities;
- floating gains cannot unlock capacity and floating losses reduce conservative capital immediately;
- deposits, withdrawals, realized P&L and balance must reconcile;
- every opportunity must still be certified, unexpired, setup-valid, cost-positive and clock-valid;
- per-trade and portfolio risk fractions remain constitutional ceilings;
- a profit goal is telemetry, never an entry condition.

This encodes the requested gain-seeking behavior under the existing hierarchy: survival, rules,
process integrity, capital preservation, then growth.

## Research-genetic sources

The implementation borrows bounded design ideas, not runtime authority:

- **Kronos:** financial K-line models as specialist challengers, never a production trading system.
- **Chronos-2:** quantile forecasts, covariates and weighted quantile evaluation.
- **Uni2TS/Moirai:** rolling probabilistic evaluation and CRPS-oriented evidence.
- **Vibe-Trading:** point-in-time splits, unit correctness, durable execution ownership and kill-switch lessons.
- **Qlib:** loose coupling between data, model, strategy, portfolio and execution.
- **RD-Agent:** hypothesis → experiment → quantitative feedback iteration.
- **TradingAgents:** point-in-time and checkpoint lessons without LLM execution authority.
- **ai-hedge-fund:** deterministic portfolio construction from bounded analyst signals.
- **Automaton:** once-per-heartbeat capital/resource state and capability shedding; replication,
  self-funding and sovereign self-modification remain rejected.
- **Microsoft Agent Framework:** durable workflow/checkpoint identity without importing the framework runtime.

## Native proof campaign

Cloud CI proves deterministic software contracts on Windows and Ubuntu. It does not prove broker or
market outcomes. Operational M95 evidence requires the following frozen campaign:

1. On the exact candidate commit, compile and run `mt5/DustySessionProbe.mq5` in the target Windows
   MT5 terminal and retain its CSV hash.
2. Bind exact broker, server, terminal build, symbol, timeframe, session export, market-data input,
   model artifact/config and forecast output.
3. Replay the same point-in-time inputs and prove deterministic forecast equality.
4. Run the already frozen M75 native indicator and Strategy Tester parity campaign without changing
   tolerances after results are visible.
5. Forward-test the frozen forecast champion across six independent Demo Desk sessions. Preserve all
   predictions before outcomes, calibration, net P&L after costs, drawdown, clock observations and rule
   violations.
6. Feed the real hashes/results into `certify_forecast_phase`. A mismatch must fail; it must not be
   “fixed” by widening thresholds after the fact.

Until those artifacts exist, the honest M95 level is `OPERATIONAL_EVIDENCE_REQUIRED`. Even an
`OPERATIONALLY_PROVEN` M95 result certifies the forecast/Demo package only; `live_write_authorized`
remains `False`.
